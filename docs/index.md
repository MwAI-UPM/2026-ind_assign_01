The **Assessment Pipeline** transforms heterogeneous Moodle submissions into
structured, reviewable evidence for cohort-level teaching insight.

It combines document extraction and normalization with deterministic text
processing, structured GPT-OSS analysis, evaluation, AI-use reporting, and
Digital Twin adoption signals. The resulting JSON and CSV products support
teacher review and can feed a teaching dashboard.

The pipeline is designed to support academic judgement, not replace it. Its
automated classifications are evidence-organizing signals and should be
reviewed in context by teaching staff.
